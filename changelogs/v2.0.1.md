## Change Log for Cuboid Outpost: Splendor Edition 2.0.1

### Changes Made / Issues Fixed

- **No more "blocked mods" when importing into the CurseForge app.** Three mods' authors have turned off CurseForge third-party distribution, so the app made you download them by hand:
  - **Spawner Bug Fix** and **More Overlays Updated** are MIT-licensed, so the pack now bundles them in `overrides/mods`, with their licences in `licenses/`.
  - **Corail Tombstone** is "All rights reserved" and can't be bundled, so it has been replaced by **Corpse**. Nothing in the quests used Tombstone.
- On death your items now stay in a **corpse** at the spot where you died. Right-click it to get them back. Tombstone's extras (perks, magic items, decorative graves) are gone.

### Upgrading an existing world

- **Before updating, empty every Tombstone grave.** Removing the mod deletes its graves and the items inside them, along with any Tombstone items or blocks you own.
- Forge will warn that the world "was saved with mod tombstone which appears to be missing". After you've emptied the graves, that warning is expected and safe.

### Mods Added

- **Corpse** - 1.16.5-1.0.7

### Mods Removed

- **Corail Tombstone** - 6.7.3-1.16.5 (its licence doesn't allow bundling, and CurseForge can't distribute it)

### Packaging

- **Spawner Bug Fix** (MIT) and **More Overlays Updated** (MIT) are now bundled in the pack instead of downloaded by CurseForge.
